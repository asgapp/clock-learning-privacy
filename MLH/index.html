Money Learning Hero

Learn Money Through Play

Money Learning Hero is a fun and interactive educational app designed to help children understand money through simple learning activities and games.

Children can practice recognizing coins and notes, counting money, making amounts, shopping, calculating change, completing speed challenges, and learning basic budgeting skills.

The app supports multiple currencies, allowing children to explore and learn different types of money.
