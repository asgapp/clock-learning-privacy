Privacy Policy

Money Learning Hero
Last updated: August 16, 2026

Money Learning Hero is an educational app designed to help children learn about money through interactive activities and games. We take privacy, particularly children's privacy, seriously.

Information We Collect

Money Learning Hero does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or home address.

The app does not collect personal information from children.

Advertising and Tracking

Money Learning Hero does not contain third-party advertising.

The app does not use advertising identifiers for tracking and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Accounts and Social Features

Money Learning Hero does not require an account or login. The app does not include messaging, chat, social networking, or user-generated content features.

In-App Purchases

Money Learning Hero does not currently contain in-app purchases.

Learning Progress

The app may store information such as learning progress, completed levels, stars, scores, achievements, selected currency, and app settings locally on the user's device so that progress can be restored when the app is opened again.

This locally stored information is used to provide the app's educational features and is not used for advertising.

Children's Privacy

Money Learning Hero is designed with children and families in mind. The app does not knowingly request personal information from children.

The app is designed to provide its core learning activities without requiring a child to submit personal information.

Third-Party Services

Money Learning Hero does not currently use third-party advertising services or social-media integrations.

External Links

The core learning experience does not require children to browse external websites. Any support or privacy information made available outside the app is intended for parents or guardians.

Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by the app and use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the app to protect its functionality and locally stored information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about Money Learning Hero or this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the contact information provided on our Support page.
