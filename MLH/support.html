Money Learning Hero Support

We're here to help.

Money Learning Hero is an educational app designed to make learning about money fun and interactive for children.

Having a Problem?

If you experience an issue with Money Learning Hero, please try closing and reopening the app first.

If the problem continues, please contact us and include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS version
  • Money Learning Hero app version
  • A short description of the problem
  • A screenshot, if helpful

Learning Progress

Money Learning Hero stores learning progress and settings on the device. Progress may be affected if the app is deleted or its locally stored data is removed.

Changing Currency

You can change the learning currency from the app's Settings. Progress may be maintained separately for supported currencies, allowing children to explore different types of money.

Feedback and Suggestions

We welcome feedback from parents and guardians. If you have a suggestion for improving Money Learning Hero, please contact us.
